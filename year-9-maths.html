Serious teaching. Visible progress.

  • Live teaching, not recorded lessons

    Lessons are taught live, in real time. Students can ask questions, teachers can check understanding and respond when something is not clear.

  • Active participation

    Students are normally expected to join lessons ready to participate, with working video and audio and a reliable internet connection so the teacher can maintain an interactive lesson.

  • More than worksheets

    Practice matters, but students also need clear explanation, questioning, feedback and help understanding their mistakes.

  • Teaching that responds to the student

    We use the diagnostic, lesson evidence and teacher judgement to understand where support is needed rather than simply giving every student the same work.

  • Clearer information for parents

    Parents should have a clearer picture of where their child is progressing and where further support may be needed.

You may be noticing that your child:

  • understands topics in class but struggles to apply them later;
  • keeps making the same mistakes;
  • has gaps from earlier Maths topics;
  • is becoming less confident as GCSE work approaches.

The first step is understanding what is actually causing the difficulty.

Understand the starting point

Start with a free initial diagnostic

The diagnostic helps us identify areas that appear secure and areas that may need more support.

Afterwards, we speak with you about what we found and what the most appropriate next step may be. The diagnostic is an initial admissions step; it does not guarantee a place.
