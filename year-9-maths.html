Why parents come to Jothi

A clearer picture before GCSE work gathers pace

Year 9 is often when gaps become easier to see. Parents may be unsure whether the foundations are secure, whether their child is being challenged appropriately, or whether progress is visible beyond completed homework.

  • Structured teaching that follows a clear academic path, rather than ad-hoc homework help.
  • Regular evidence of what is secure, what still needs attention and what should happen next.
  • Appropriate challenge in a small group where the student can participate and be known.
  • Calm, direct feedback that helps parents understand progress before Year 10.

How the starter works

A clear first four lessons

  1. 1

    Free diagnostic

    The initial diagnostic is free. We use it to understand your child’s current level and identify a suitable group.

  2. 2

    Confirm the place

    We confirm a suitable group, lesson dates and availability before payment. Places are available in selected groups, subject to a suitable match.

  3. 3

    Start with four lessons

    4 × 1-hour online small-group lessons for £100.

  4. 4

    Review progress and fit

    After four lessons, we review progress and whether the group is working well.

  5. 5

    Continue if it works

    Continue at £25/hr or choose an agreed fixed monthly arrangement. There is no automatic monthly conversion.

Any later assessment is discussed separately and does not replace a teaching lesson. Unused prepaid lessons are refunded if you decide to stop.
