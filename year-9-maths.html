Why parents come to Jothi

A clearer picture before GCSE work gathers pace

Year 9 is often when gaps become easier to see. Parents may be unsure whether the foundations are secure, whether their child is being challenged appropriately, or whether progress is visible beyond completed homework.

  • Structured teaching that follows a clear academic path, rather than ad-hoc homework help.
  • Regular evidence of what is secure, what still needs attention and what should happen next.
  • Appropriate challenge in a small group where the student can participate and be known.
  • Calm, direct feedback that helps parents understand progress before Year 10.

How the programme works

A consistent teaching and assessment rhythm

01

Weekly teaching

One live 60-minute Maths lesson each week.

02

Matched small groups

Groups are usually 2–3 students and never more than 4. Students are carefully matched by current level, learning pace and academic ambition.

03

Regular assessment

Eight regular assessments across the programme help teachers and parents understand progress and remaining gaps.

04

Parent feedback

Regular progress tracking and parent feedback, plus at least two parent consultations during the programme.

How joining works

Consultation, diagnostic, matched placement

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We first understand the student’s current position and what the parent is looking for.

  2. 2

    Diagnostic

    Every student completes a diagnostic session before placement. This is included in the programme fee and helps us understand current strengths, gaps and a suitable teaching group. The diagnostic also helps us decide whether catch-up lessons are needed before regular group placement.

  3. 3

    Matched group placement

    We place the student only when there is an appropriate group based on current level and teaching pace.

If the diagnostic shows that a student needs more sustained support than the standard programme provides, Jothi discusses this with the parent and agrees an appropriate pathway before making any additional commitment.

Programme fee

Clear programme pricing

The full September–July Year 9 Maths programme is £1,045 per subject.

It includes 42 live lessons, 8 regular assessments, progress tracking, parent updates, the diagnostic used for placement and at least two parent consultations.

Joining after September?

We first review the student’s current level and the time remaining in the programme. Where needed, we may schedule extra lessons in the first few weeks to help them catch up.

We then confirm the appropriate programme fee and payment plan before enrolment.

The final fee may be adjusted up or down depending on the student’s current knowledge, the support required and the remaining teaching period.
