# SATs Maths Learning Roadmap — Academic Review Guide

**Reviewers:** Sruthi and Radhika
**Review status:** Independent academic review pending
**Audience:** Parents of Year 6 pupils; this is an educational guide, not an independent diagnostic assessment.

## Purpose and review approach

The roadmap presents eight areas of Maths through 60 parent-facing topic cards. Each card contains an illustration, a plain-English explanation and a reasoning connection. Review these separately:

- **Illustration:** mathematical accuracy, scale, notation, representations and whether the diagram could mislead.
- **Explanation:** factual accuracy, clarity and suitability for a parent-facing guide.
- **Reasoning prompt:** whether it meaningfully connects to the topic without becoming a diagnostic claim.

For every topic, use one status only:

- **APPROVED** — academically accurate and clear as presented.
- **CORRECTION REQUIRED** — an error or misleading presentation needs amendment.
- **DISCUSSION REQUIRED** — accuracy may be defensible, but framing, classification or teaching clarity needs agreement.
- **NOT YET REVIEWED** — default status; no academic sign-off implied.

Please identify mathematical mistakes, unclear written methods, incorrect Year 5/6 classification, omitted or misleading concepts, and material gaps in the overall statutory-coverage picture. The 60 illustrated topics do **not** themselves establish complete statutory coverage.
