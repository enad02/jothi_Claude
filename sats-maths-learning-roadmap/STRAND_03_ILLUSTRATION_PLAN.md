# Strand 3 — Fractions illustration plan

All examples are proposals for review. They do not alter the approved public curriculum wording or mark a topic as academically approved.

| ID | Teaching objective | Proposed worked example | Exact visual representation | Accuracy checks | Best method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T01` | Recognise a fraction as equal parts of one whole. | `3/4`: three of four equal parts. | One equal-width four-part bar, three parts gold-highlighted; labelled numerator and denominator. | One defined whole; all four areas equal; shaded count is 3; denominator is 4. | Deterministic SVG |
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T02` | Simplify an equivalent fraction using a common factor. | `6/8 = 3/4`. | Two same-length bars: eight equal eighths above, four equal quarters below, aligned to equivalent lengths. | Every bar has one whole; `6/8` and `3/4` shade the same proportion; division by 2 applies to numerator and denominator. | Deterministic SVG |
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T03` | Compare fractions by size. | `3/4 > 5/8`. | Two equal-length bars with exact quarter and eighth partitions; comparison symbol centred between them. | Bars represent equal wholes; shaded lengths are exactly 75% and 62.5%; no unequal visual scaling. | Deterministic SVG |
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T04` | Add and subtract fractions using equal-sized parts. | Year 5: `1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5`; Year 6 progression: `1/3 + 1/6 = 1/2`. | A same-denominator bar sequence, then an optional equivalent-sixths conversion strip. | Year 5 example keeps denominator fixed; Year 6 conversion changes both parts to sixths before combining. | Hybrid illustration |
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T05` | Add and subtract mixed numbers. | `1 1/2 + 2 1/4 = 3 3/4`. | Three complete wholes plus an aligned halves/quarters fraction strip. | Whole-number and fractional portions remain distinct; `1/2` is visibly converted to `2/4`; result is less than 4. | Deterministic SVG |
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T06` | Multiply fractions, distinguishing a fraction of a whole from a fraction of a fraction. | Year 5: `3 × 1/4 = 3/4`; Year 6 progression: `2/3 × 3/4 = 1/2`. | Repeated quarter strips, then a 3-by-4 area model with two rows and three columns highlighted. | Year 5 is whole-number scaling; Year 6 model has 12 equal cells and six overlap cells; simplification is correct. | Deterministic SVG |
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T07` | Divide a proper fraction by a whole number. | `3/4 ÷ 2 = 3/8`. | A bar split into four quarters, with each shaded quarter bisected precisely to show two equal shares. | Division means equal sharing; eight equal eighths result; three eighths is half of three quarters. | Deterministic SVG |
| `SAT-MATH-S03-T08` | Find a fraction of an amount. | `3/5 of 25 = 15`. | 25 counters in five equal groups of five; three groups highlighted. | Groups have equal size; denominator gives five groups; numerator selects three; answer is 15. | Hybrid illustration |

## Year 5 / Year 6 progression boundary

Topics `T04` and `T06` deliberately show a Year 5 accessible starting relationship before introducing the Year 6 extension. They do not imply that each proposed example is expected at both year levels.
