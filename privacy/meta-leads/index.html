Privacy Notice

Meta Lead Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Jothi Learning uses personal information submitted through Meta lead forms, including Facebook and Instagram lead forms.

Data controller: JOTHI LEARNING LIMITED, 2 Wilmot Drive, Birmingham, B23 5UA.

Notice details

This page applies to personal information submitted through Jothi Learning Meta lead forms.

  • Meta lead-form enquiries
  • Parent consultations and initial diagnostics
  • Contact: legal@jothi.uk

Who we are

Jothi Learning is responsible for this website and the personal data collected through it. For privacy queries, you can contact Jothi Learning at legal@jothi.uk.

Information we collect through Meta lead forms

Depending on the form you complete, we may collect a parent or guardian's name, email address, mobile number, student first name and surname, student year group, subject of interest, preferred contact method, Maths concerns or other enquiry information. We may also receive Meta lead identifiers and campaign or form attribution information.

Why we use this information

  • To respond to your enquiry and assess suitability for Year 9 Maths support.
  • To arrange a parent consultation and, where appropriate, discuss or arrange an initial diagnostic.
  • To manage the enquiry through Jothi Learning's admissions process, maintain CRM records and follow up.
  • To understand which marketing routes are generating enquiries.

Systems and service providers

Information submitted through a Meta lead form may pass through Meta, Jothi Learning's CRM and integration systems, and relevant service providers that support enquiry handling. These providers should only receive the information needed for their role.

We do not sell your personal information.

Lawful basis

Jothi Learning uses enquiry details because it is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a service arrangement, and because Jothi Learning has a legitimate interest in managing and responding to genuine enquiries.

Marketing communications

Submitting an enquiry does not automatically add you to a marketing email list. Any separate marketing consent is handled separately.

Retention

Enquiries should only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, assess fit, and keep a basic record of interest. If no service starts, Jothi Learning aims to delete or review enquiry-stage data within 12 months unless there is a valid reason to keep a basic record for longer.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, request correction, request erasure in some cases, object to certain uses, or raise a concern. You can contact Jothi Learning first at legal@jothi.uk.

ICO registration

JOTHI LEARNING LIMITED is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office under registration reference ZC121472. Registration date: 12 April 2026. Registration expires: 11 April 2027.
