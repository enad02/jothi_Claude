The reasoning challenge

Knowing the method is one thing. Knowing when to use it is another.

A pupil may perform known calculations but need help recognising which Maths knowledge a worded problem requires. Missing prior knowledge can also block reasoning. Effective SATs preparation needs both secure understanding and practice applying it in unfamiliar contexts.

How it works

How SATs Maths Mastery works in practice

Teaching decisions are informed by what pupils show in their work—not only the final mark.

Topic checks show which methods are secure and which areas need closer attention.

Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions

How is reasoning different from routine Maths?

Reasoning often asks pupils to recognise which knowledge is useful, connect steps and explain a route through an unfamiliar question.

Will familiar topics be repeated?

The programme follows a structured sequence. Topic checks and assessment evidence help teachers decide where understanding needs consolidation or further reasoning practice.

What if my child has significant gaps?

The free initial assessment helps us discuss whether the core programme is appropriate. A different pathway may be recommended where significant foundational gaps are identified.

How much does the programme cost?

The fixed programme fee is £900, payable in six instalments of £150. It is not a rolling monthly subscription.

What device does my child need?

A pen-enabled tablet or equivalent suitable interactive-writing device is required. Please discuss an existing device with us if you are unsure.

Can families enquire after the programme has started?

Late joining is assessed individually; an enquiry does not guarantee a place or assessment appointment.
